# 10. Home Atmos studio

[Start here](00-start-here.md)

The record-mode diagram calls the room a home Atmos 7.1.4 room: Dolby Atmos Renderer and the Dolby Atmos Music Panner. [Atmos integration](../atmos-integration.md). [Renderer](https://professional.dolby.com/product/dolby-atmos-content-creation/dolby-atmos-renderer/).

Live cannot author Atmos objects or write an ADM BWF master. Record stems at home on Mini A from the XR18 USB 18x18, then mix in the Renderer. On macOS, Dolby Audio Bridge connects Live and the Renderer on one computer, up to 128 inputs.

To feed the PlayAUDIO1U, render a stereo or binaural re-render, or export tracks from Live with Include Return and Main Effects, at one sample rate.

Dock the case at home with one Ethernet uplink. The 4U case has no front I/O panel. Keep `192.168.1.x` off the home router. The existing rack at home stays the record-mode hub.

Recall XR18 scene REC-STUDIO. Turn AbleNet and LifeSine off. Connect RTP if Mini B must follow Mini A’s clock. [Modes](../modes-and-checklists.md).

**Unverified:** speaker angles and which interface feeds the 7.1.4 monitors. The plan only names the room in the diagram.
