## Subtotal

Verified-price items in the compact build (case, power, mount, switch, panel, Enttec, AbleSet Standard, IOGEAR KVM, monitor, Pelican, XR18 ears) come to about $1,538 by my addition. That excludes Lightkey, cables, video extenders, IEMs and the stage case.

## IEM table

1RU wireless is assumed to be IEM transmitters and is not confirmed. These options replace it. Detail is in [IEM mixes](iem-mixes.md).

| Tier | Pick | Price | Size and fit | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Mid | Sennheiser XSW IEM Set (B band) | $469 Thomann, $549 Guitar Center, $658 Sweetwater | 200 x 128 x 42 mm, 0.7 kg each, rack kit included | 572 to 596 MHz. Five sets means five units, about 5U if racked. |
